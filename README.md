# AYFED

**Personalized Health Intelligence**

Your Health. Your Data. Shared Intelligence.

AYFED is a research-driven platform for longitudinal blood test
analysis and privacy-preserving Federated Learning across laboratories.

## Vision

AYFED aims to make laboratory health data more understandable,
structured, and useful over time while preserving data privacy.

## Initial Product

AYFED Blood Test Intelligence

The initial product focuses on:

- Blood test report ingestion
- Laboratory result extraction
- Biomarker standardization
- Reference range interpretation
- Longitudinal health tracking
- Personalized health insights

## Research Direction

Laboratory-Aware Personalized Federated Learning for
Privacy-Preserving Longitudinal Health Intelligence.

The research investigates learning across heterogeneous
laboratory environments without centrally collecting raw
laboratory data.

## Architecture

The project is organized around five major components:

1. Personal Health Platform
2. Laboratory Infrastructure
3. Federated Learning Infrastructure
4. Privacy and Security
5. Research and Evaluation

## Project Status

Early foundation and architecture stage.

## Privacy

No real patient records, identifiable health information,
credentials, or confidential laboratory data should be committed
to this repository.

Synthetic and appropriately licensed research datasets
must be used for development and experimentation.

## Disclaimer

AYFED is a research and software development project.
It is not currently a validated medical device and does not
provide medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
